# Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

This project explores employee attrition patterns using Python and data analysis techniques. The objective is to identify workforce characteristics associated with employee turnover and highlight potential areas for further investigation.

## Business Objectives

Analyze the overall employee attrition rate.

Explore attrition patterns across job roles.

Investigate the relationship between overtime and employee turnover.

Examine attrition patterns by business travel frequency.

Identify workforce segments that may require further HR analysis.

## Tools & Technologies

**Python** – data analysis

**Pandas** – data manipulation and analysis

**NumPy** – numerical operations

**Matplotlib** – data visualization

**Seaborn** – statistical visualization

**Jupyter Notebook** – interactive analysis

## Key Areas of Analysis

### 1. Employee Attrition

Explore the overall proportion of employees who left the organization.

### 2. Job Role Analysis

Compare attrition patterns across different job roles to identify areas that may need further investigation.

### 3. Overtime Analysis

Explore the relationship between overtime and employee attrition.

### 4. Business Travel Analysis

Examine how employee turnover varies across business travel categories.

## Key Findings

The project documentation reports an overall employee attrition rate of 16.12%. The Sales Representative job role has a reported attrition rate of 39.76%.

These findings highlight differences in attrition across workforce segments and can help guide further investigation into employee retention.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the use of Python for exploratory data analysis, workforce trend analysis, and data visualization. The findings can support further investigation into employee retention and workforce management.
